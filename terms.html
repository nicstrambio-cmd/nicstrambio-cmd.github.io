No public hosted service, account registration or subscription is offered through this site. These terms describe this personal installation; no commercial or multi-user service is offered.

Authorized use

Use the installation only with accounts and schedules the operator is authorized to access and process. A change to Google OAuth publishing status does not transfer ownership of the installation or confer permission to use another person's credentials. Do not distribute access tokens, API keys, credential-store contents or private timetable files.

How the automation behaves

A preview reads sources, checks calendars and may submit a new timetable image to Gemini; it makes no calendar changes or report emails. An apply run can create, update and remove agent-managed teaching events and email summaries to the authenticated account. PDF lessons take priority over overlapping image lessons. Conflicts with other appointments are reported without automatically moving those appointments.

Accuracy and review

Timetable extraction can fail or produce inaccurate results, particularly with changed layouts, unclear images or incomplete source information. Review initial previews, changes and error reports against the original timetable. A report that conflict checking was incomplete must not be treated as confirmation that the schedule is clear.

Availability and third-party services

Scheduled operation depends on the computer, Windows session, internet connection, valid authorization and the availability of Google services. Missed schedules, expired credentials and service failures can prevent updates. Google and other chosen providers apply their own service terms, quotas and charges. The operator manages those accounts and settings.

Privacy and stopping use

The privacy notice describes the implementation's data handling. Stop use by disabling the task and revoking relevant authorizations. Existing calendar events, messages, local files and cloud copies require separate deletion when no longer needed.

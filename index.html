Personal teaching administration

Your teaching timetable,
in your calendar.

Work Timetable Agent helps its operator turn weekly teaching schedules into Google Calendar events and review changes and overlaps.

01 / READ

Weekly schedules

Reads matching Gmail timetable emails and PDF attachments. It also accepts timetable images saved manually from WhatsApp.

02 / ORGANIZE

One work calendar

Creates and updates teaching events. PDF lessons take priority over overlapping image lessons; other image lessons are retained.

03 / REVIEW

Clear reports

Shows changes, skipped image lessons, other calendar conflicts and errors. Reports are saved locally and can be emailed to the operator.

A private desktop installation

The program runs on the operator's computer. It is not offered as a public hosted service, and this information site provides no registration, timetable upload or access to the operator's calendar.

Connected services

The operator grants access through Google OAuth. Gmail supplies timetable messages; Google Calendar stores events. Optional image extraction sends manually selected timetable images to the Gemini API. The program does not connect to WhatsApp chats.

Understand how information is handled. The privacy notice describes permissions, local files, cloud services and deletion. The terms of use describe the intended use and limitations.
